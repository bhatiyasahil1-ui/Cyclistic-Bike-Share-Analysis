# 🚴 Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis (Google Data Analytics Capstone)

## 📌 Business Overview
Cyclistic aims to maximize annual memberships for business growth. This analysis evaluates historical trip data to understand how **annual members** and **casual riders** use Cyclistic bikes differently, providing data-driven strategies to convert casual riders into loyal members.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib):** Large-scale data cleaning, feature engineering, and descriptive analysis.
* **Google Sheets:** Pivot table logic validation, summary statistics, and executive bar chart visualization.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Analytical Findings
* **Usage Patterns:** Members exhibit consistent daily usage focused on weekday commute hours (shorter, fixed durations ~14 mins).
* **Leisure & Weekend Spike:** Casual riders show high weekend activity with significantly longer trip durations (~60 mins).
* **Conversion Opportunity:** Marketing campaigns targeted at weekend casual riders offering conversion incentives for annual plans.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
1. Offer seasonal weekend-to-annual membership conversion discounts.
2. Target digital marketing campaigns at peak casual usage hours during weekends.
3. Introduce commuter loyalty rewards to encourage daily usage among casual users.
